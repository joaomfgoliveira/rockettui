## Phase Goal

Roadmap goal (verbatim): The TUI is customizable through three TOML files in `~/.config/rockettui/`, with built-in defaults.

User story: not framed (see plan 02-01).

This plan makes the remaining config.toml settings visible: plan 02-01 parses and validates them, this plan feeds them to the layout, the message renderer and the run loop. It runs in parallel with plan 02-03 (keymap.toml); the two plans share no file.

Parallel-safety note: plan 02-03 runs at the same time and never edits src/app/mod.rs, src/lib.rs, src/test_util.rs or any src/ui file; this plan never edits src/config/*, src/input/*, src/action.rs, src/app/update.rs or scripts/smoke_pty.py.

<objective>
Drive the sidebar width, the message timestamp format and the key-sequence timeout from config.toml, keeping the reducer's geometry and row model identical to what the renderer draws.

Purpose: the user-visible half of CONF-07 (ROADMAP success criterion 4) and the D-12/D-13 settings; Phase 1 D-06 (timeout) and D-13 (fixed sidebar) become configurable.
Output: `layout::areas` with a width parameter, `App::areas`, src/ui/timefmt.rs, `wrap::RowCtx` + `time_column`, `App::row_ctx`, `App::key_timeout`, local time in the binary, and two reviewed full-screen snapshots of non-default configs.
</objective>
