## Phase Goal

Roadmap goal (verbatim): User authenticates with a PAT and reads real rooms and history from a local Docker Rocket.Chat.

User story: not framed (see plan 03-01).

Output: the reconnect command, history::retry_failed, an error-mapping and transport test set, a cfg(test) redaction module, and the unauthorized pty scenario.
</objective>
